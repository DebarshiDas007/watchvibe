WATCHVIBE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

WatchVibe ("the app", "we") is a free movie and TV discovery app at watchvibe-b92.pages.dev. This page explains what we collect and why. You can use the app as a guest without signing in.

What we collect

  • Account details (only if you sign in): your email address, display name and profile picture URL, provided by email sign-in, Google or Discord.
  • Your activity: your watchlist, watched history and the vibes you pick for titles, so they can sync across your devices.
  • On your device: the app stores preferences, your guest watchlist and cached catalog data in your browser's local storage.
  • Approximate region: to show streaming availability for your country, your browser's time zone and language are used, and a request to a public geolocation service (ipapi.co or api.country.is) is made, which sees your IP address.
  • Anonymous usage statistics: to understand what works and fix problems, the app records simple events (for example a title opened, a search term, a streaming link clicked, a trailer that failed to play, or an error) together with a random anonymous visitor id kept in your browser, your country, device type (mobile, tablet or desktop), browser name and the site you came from. These records contain no email address, name or IP address and are not linked to your account. They are deleted after about 90 to 365 days. If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, nothing is recorded.

How we use it

Only to run and improve the app: signing you in, syncing your watchlist, showing streaming options for your region, personalising recommendations, and understanding overall usage (for example which titles are missing or broken). We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for advertising.

Sign-in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", we receive only your basic profile (name, email address and profile picture) with your consent. WatchVibe's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase: authentication and database for accounts and synced watchlists.
  • Cloudflare: hosting and delivery of the app.
  • TMDB: movie and TV metadata and images (requests are made from your browser).
  • JustWatch: our server asks JustWatch which streaming service carries a title in your country, to link you to the right page. Only the title and country are sent, nothing about you.
  • Google / Discord: only if you choose those sign-in options.

Retention and your choices

Your account data is kept until you ask us to delete it. You can sign out at any time, and clearing your browser's site data removes everything stored on your device. To delete your account and synced data, or to ask what we hold about you, email dasdebarshi1234@gmail.com.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS and access to account data is restricted per user in the database. No system is perfectly secure, so please use the app at your own discretion.

Children

WatchVibe is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
